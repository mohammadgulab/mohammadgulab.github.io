A WELL OF
FLOUR.

Every shape on the menu starts the same way: a mound of semola, a well in the middle, and eggs whisked in with a fork.

Chef Rania Haddad learned it in a basement kitchen in Bologna and brought it to Al Rawdah in 2021. Twelve seats at the counter, one long table, and a pasta room behind glass where you can watch the morning shift roll.
