    02 The method

    Ninety minutes. Not one shortcut.

    1. 01

      Marinate

      Chicken rests overnight in yogurt, ginger, garlic and our own masala.

    2. 02

      Par-boil

      Long grain basmati, boiled with whole spices until it is almost there.

    3. 03

      Layer

      Rice over meat, then saffron milk, fried onions, mint and ghee.

    4. 04

      Seal and dum

      The handi is sealed with dough and left over low coals. Nobody opens it early.
