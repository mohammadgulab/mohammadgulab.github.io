Questions, answered

Do I need to provide cleaning products?

No. Your cleaner brings eco friendly products, microfibre cloths, a mop and a vacuum. If you prefer your own products, just tell us.

Are your cleaners vetted?

Every cleaner is interviewed, ID checked, trained in house for two weeks and insured while working in your home.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 12 hours before your booking, from the link in your confirmation message.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Jeddah, from Obhur and Al Shati in the north to Al Hamdaniyah and Al Safa.

How do I pay?

Card, Apple Pay or cash after the clean. Regular plans are billed monthly.
