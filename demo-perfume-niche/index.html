The house

Made slowly, shown simply

Hajar means stone. Our room on the Corniche is a single wall of carved niches, one bottle to each, so every scent gets its own light and its own moment before you try it on skin.

Each perfume is blended in small batches and rested before it is bottled. Ask for a blotter of any of the five, or sit down for a quiet consultation and leave with one that suits you.

  • Small batchesBlended and rested in the atelier
  • Oils and spraysConcentrated attar and eau de parfum
