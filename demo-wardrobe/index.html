Photos never do a wardrobe room justice. Walking into one does.

We design, build and install walk-in wardrobes for homes across Jeddah. Every room is drawn around what you own: how many abayas and thobes, how many pairs of shoes, where the watches live. Then we build it in our workshop and fit it in three days.

The system

Three layers, one room

01

Rails & frame

Powder coated steel or solid oak uprights, double hanging where you need it, long drop for dresses.

02

Drawers, trays, shoes

Soft close drawers, velvet lined trays for watches and rings, tilted shoe shelves that show every pair.

03

Light

Warm LED lines in every shelf and rail, on a motion sensor, so the room wakes up as you walk in.

How it works

From empty room
to dressing room

  1. Design visit

    We measure the room and count what you own. Free within Jeddah.

  2. 3D plan

    A walkthrough of your room with finishes and lighting, revised until it is right.

  3. Workshop build

    Every panel is cut and finished in our Al Khumrah workshop.

  4. Three day fit

    Installed, lit and cleaned. We even hang the first rail for you.
