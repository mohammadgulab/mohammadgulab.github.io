How we work

A process you can follow, step by step.

  1. 01

    Brief & feasibility

    Site visit, scope, budget range and a realistic programme before anything is signed.

  2. 02

    Design coordination

    Architectural, structural and MEP drawings checked together, so clashes are solved on paper.

  3. 03

    Procurement & precast

    Materials submitted and approved early; precast elements scheduled to arrive when the crane is ready.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Daily site supervision, inspection requests and progress reports you can actually read.

  5. 05

    Handover

    Snagging, testing and a complete as-built and O&M package, organised and indexed.
