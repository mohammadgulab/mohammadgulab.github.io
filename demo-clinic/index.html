FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to book before coming?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking a slot means little or no waiting. You can book online in under a minute.

What is the consultation fee?

In this demo the clinic visit is SAR 150 and a video consultation is SAR 100. A real clinic would show its own fees here.

Do you accept insurance?

This demo shows where an insurance list would go. A live site would list the insurance companies the clinic works with.

Can I get my reports on my phone?

Yes. Lab reports and prescriptions appear in the patient app under My Reports, and you can download them as PDF.

Which languages does the doctor speak?

English, Hindi, Urdu and Arabic.

How do I cancel or change my appointment?

Open the patient app, tap your upcoming appointment and choose Reschedule or Cancel. You can also send a WhatsApp message.
