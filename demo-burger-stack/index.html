// Where it began

One griddle. One rule.

Stackhouse started as a weekend stall with a single hot plate and one rule: smash it fresh, serve it hot. The story below is part of this concept demo.

  1. The stallWeekend nights, one griddle, a queue around the corner.
  2. The recipeHouse sauce settled after a hundred late night tastings.
  3. The shopA small counter in Jeddah with the same hot plate energy.
  4. TodayStill smashed to order. Still one flip.
