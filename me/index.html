About

I like things that are clear, tidy and easy to find. On site that means every drawing, submittal and inspection request in the right place. On screen it means a brand, a page or a sheet that says exactly what it should.

I am Mohammad Gulab Kauser, and people call me Kaish. I work as a Document Controller at Qanbar Dywidag Precast Concrete Co. Ltd in Jeddah, where I handle the paperwork that keeps precast concrete moving from the plant to the site.

I studied Physics at Jai Prakash University, learned React at DUCAT, and learned design through practice and a Photoshop and Illustrator course. Today I design for small businesses and make Excel templates that I sell online.
